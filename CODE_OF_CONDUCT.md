# Code of Conduct

TabPlex welcomes focused, respectful participation from people of different
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and its user impact, not the person proposing it.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good intent while asking for
  evidence.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples instead of browsing history, private
  URLs, exported backups, credentials, or Agent connection material.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to narrow, postpone, or decline a change when
  it conflicts with product safety or project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private data are not accepted in
project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `dvdx@foxmail.com`. Do not use a public
issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately as practical;
conflicts of interest should be disclosed and delegated when possible.

This policy applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and other
spaces where someone represents the TabPlex project.
